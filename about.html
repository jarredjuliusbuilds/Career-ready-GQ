About CareerReady Gqeberha

Our mission and the measurable objectives we're working toward.

Our Mission

Our mission is to sell what most CV services don't have: real, current knowledge of what employers in Nelson Mandela Bay are actually looking for, and the skill to help you display — and enhance — what you already bring to the table. That knowledge is why clients choose us, not simply because we can put words on a page. Whether you're applying for a factory floor role, a technical trade at Coega, or any other job in the city, we know what gets noticed and what gets skipped, and we build your application around that. Every client walks away with a complete, ready-to-use package, handed directly to you: your finished application alongside an interview sheet with the questions you're likely to be asked, and the smart questions to ask your employer in return — the kind that shows you're serious about the job. We do this with honesty, fair local pricing, and the belief that being overlooked is a presentation problem, not a skills problem.

Our Objectives

These are the specific, measurable goals we're working toward — and how we'll know we're keeping our promise to clients.

  1. Deliver every CV and interview prep pack within the promised turnaround (2–3 working days for Factory-Ready, 3–5 for Coega-Ready) for at least 95% of orders.
  2. Respond to every WhatsApp enquiry within 4 business hours during operating hours (Mon–Fri 08:00–18:00, Sat 09:00–13:00).
  3. Complete at least 25 client CVs in the first 3 months of operation, including free testimonial clients, to build a proven local track record.
  4. Maintain a client satisfaction rating of 4.5 out of 5 or higher, based on feedback collected after every delivery.
  5. Grow to 1,000 local Instagram followers within 6 months through consistent value, proof, and local job-opportunity content.
  6. Introduce a 30-minute mock interview add-on and move Coega-Ready pricing to R550–R650 once 10 or more verified client testimonials are collected.
  7. Build a working relationship with at least one local partner — the NMB Business Chamber, Coega Development Corporation, or a local recruitment agency — within the first year to reach more job seekers directly.
  8. Launch a downloadable "Artisan's Interview Playbook" digital product within 12 months as a low-cost, always-available resource beyond one-on-one clients.
