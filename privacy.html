Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026. This policy explains how CareerReady Gqeberha collects, uses, and protects your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

1. Introduction

CareerReady Gqeberha ('we', 'us', 'our') is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect when you use our services, why we collect it, how we use and protect it, and what rights you have under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

2. Information We Collect

To prepare your CV, cover letter, and interview materials, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address
  • Your work history, education, qualifications, and skills
  • The job title, industry, or job advertisement you are applying to
  • Any other information you choose to share with us to help tailor your CV and interview preparation

We do not collect payment card details or bank account numbers. EFT payments are made directly between your bank and ours, and we do not store your banking information.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Prepare and deliver your CV, cover letter, and interview preparation materials
  • Communicate with you about your order, including confirming details and handling revisions
  • Improve our services and templates over time (using anonymised information only, never your personal details)

We do not use your information for any purpose beyond delivering the service you've ordered, unless you separately agree to something else (for example, being asked for a testimonial).

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your information may only be shared where required by law, or with your explicit consent (for example, if you ask us to share a testimonial using your name).

5. Communication via WhatsApp

Much of our communication with you takes place over WhatsApp. WhatsApp is operated by Meta, and messages sent through it are subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy and security practices, which are outside of our control. We recommend reviewing WhatsApp's privacy policy separately if you have concerns about how the platform itself handles your data.

6. How Long We Keep Your Information

We retain your CV and related information for as long as necessary to deliver your order and to honour the free revision period (7 days after delivery). After this, we may keep a copy on file for a reasonable period in case you return for future updates, unless you ask us to delete it sooner (see Section 8, Your Rights).

7. How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information from loss, misuse, or unauthorised access, in line with the security requirements set out in POPIA. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission (including WhatsApp and email) is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights Under POPIA

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act, you have the right to:

  • Ask us what personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct any inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete your personal information, subject to any legal or legitimate business reasons we may need to retain it
  • Object to how we process your information
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa) if you believe your information has been mishandled

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 11 below.

9. Cookies and Website Data

This website does not currently use cookies, analytics, or third-party tracking of any kind. If this changes in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for job seekers who are legally permitted to work in South Africa. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and our services are not directed at children.

11. Contact Us / Information Officer

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, or would like to exercise any of your rights under POPIA, please contact us via WhatsApp at wa.me/27678409547 or by email at hello@careerreadygqeberha.co.za. You may also contact the Information Regulator (South Africa) directly at inforeg.org.za if you are not satisfied with our response.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised 'last updated' date.
