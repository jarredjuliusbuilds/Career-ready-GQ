Our Mission & Objective

Our Mission

Get every client in the room.

CareerReady Gqeberha exists because good candidates get overlooked before anyone reads their story. We sell market knowledge first — knowing what local employers look for, and how to display and enhance the skills you already have — and apply it to your application, whatever job you're going for.

Our Objective

Close the gap between skill and selection.

Nelson Mandela Bay has thousands of capable people and factories that still can't fill vacancies. That's not a skills gap — it's a presentation gap. We close it, one CV and one interview prep sheet at a time, for Coega's technical roles and the city's factory floors alike.
